The evidence workflow fixes the input and reference-label hashes, prompt hash, prediction
schema, paired statistical tests, and claim-to-evidence mapping. It fail-closes on missing
diagnostic predictions, unmeasured model-size values, unresolved evidence fields, or stale
commit provenance. The current primary-run artifacts do not establish production
validation, human ground truth, safe autonomous blocking, or universal model superiority;
the complete evidence claim remains blocked until the predeclared inference-path
diagnostics and strict validation pass.
